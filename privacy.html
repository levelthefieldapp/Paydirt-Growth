Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Paydirt Growth ("Paydirt," "we," "us") makes Paydirt Watchdog, which checks trade businesses' Google Ads accounts for wasted spend, and also manages Google marketing for some trade businesses. This policy explains what we collect through paydirtgrowth.com, Paydirt Watchdog, and our other services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you give us when you ask for a free Leak Report: your name, email, business name, and, if you choose, your trade, monthly ad spend, and who runs your ads.
  • What you give us when you book a call: your name, mobile number, email, business name, and the time you pick.
  • Google Ads account data: when you add audits@paydirtgrowth.com to your Google Ads account, we can see that account's settings and performance data, such as campaigns, keywords, search terms, ad schedules, locations, budgets, spend, clicks, and conversions. We get read-only access. We don't receive your Google password.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe, our payment processor collects your card details. We see the last four digits and billing status, not your full card number.
  • Messages: texts, emails, and call records between you and us.
  • Website usage: pages viewed, the link or ad that brought you here, device and browser type, and approximate location. We may use analytics and advertising tools such as Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and the Meta Pixel, which use cookies or similar technology.
  • Client account data: if you become a client, we access the accounts you connect (for example Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Jobber, or Quo) only as needed to do the work in our agreement.

How we use it

  • To find wasted spend in your Google Ads account and send you your Leak Report, Monday emails, alerts, and report cards.
  • To schedule calls you book and follow up about them.
  • To compare your numbers with your own history and, once there are enough accounts, with anonymous averages for businesses in the same trade and market. We never show your business's name or numbers to another customer.
  • To reply to you and provide the services you sign up for.
  • To understand how people find and use our site and to improve our marketing.
  • To keep records we're required to keep and to protect against fraud or misuse.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we'll text you about your call and your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data is never shared with any third party, except the providers that deliver our messages on our behalf.

Your Google Ads data

We use data from your Google Ads account only to provide Paydirt Watchdog to you: to find waste, recommend fixes, and report results. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove our access anytime in Google Ads under Admin, then Access and security. When you cancel, we delete your account data within 90 days unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that process it for us, under their own privacy and security terms. These include Google (Workspace, Calendar, Meet, Analytics, and the Google Ads API), Anthropic (AI used to analyze account data and help write reports and emails), Adspirer (Google Ads data access for early reports), Supabase (database), Vercel (app hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery), Quo (calls and texts), GitHub (website hosting), and our accountant. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep Leak Report requests, call bookings, and messages for as long as they're useful for following up with you, generally up to three years. We keep Google Ads account data while your Watchdog account is active and delete it within 90 days after you cancel or remove our access, and client records for as long as our agreement lasts plus whatever period tax and legal rules require. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Email us to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as limiting who can access your information, to protect it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Our site and services are for business owners. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
