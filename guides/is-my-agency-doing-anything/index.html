How to tell if your Google Ads agency is doing anything

You pay every month and get a report. Here is how to see for yourself whether anyone touched the account, in about 15 minutes, without learning Google Ads.

Count the changes

In Google Ads, type "Change history" in the search bar at the top and open it. Set the date range to the last 30 days.

Look at how many changes were made and who made them. A small account doesn't need daily changes, but a month with almost nothing is worth a question. Two or more months with nothing at all means nobody is watching.

Read the search terms

Search for "Search terms" and open the report for the last 30 days. These are the actual searches people typed before clicking your ad.

If you see jobs, DIY, other cities or services you don't offer near the top, the negative keyword list isn't being kept up. Our free lists by trade are a good starting point.

Check what counts as a lead

Search for "Conversions" and open the summary. Look at which actions are set as primary goals.

Page views, clicks on your phone number, or "engaged visitors" counted as leads will make results look better than they are. A lead should be a real call of a set length or a submitted form.

Check where ads show

Open a campaign's settings, then Locations, then Location options.

It should say "Presence: People in or regularly in your included locations." The other option also shows ads to people who are only interested in your area, which is how you end up paying for clicks from other states.

Check who owns the account

Search for "Access and security." You should be listed as an Admin on your own account.

If the agency owns the account and you only have view access, ask to change that. If you ever leave, the account and its history should stay with you.

Questions to ask your agency

Ask them as questions, not accusations. A good agency will be glad you asked.

  • What did you change in the account last month, and why?
  • How often do you review the search terms report?
  • Which actions count as a lead, and how do you know they are real?
  • What did a booked job cost us last month?

Google Ads menus move around. If you can't find something, type its name in the search bar at the top of Google Ads.
