Questions

What owners ask first.

01Who writes all the messages?
We do. After a kickoff call to learn your business, we write every text, email, and follow-up in your voice. You approve everything before it goes live.
02Which apps does this work with?
We work inside the tools you already run your business on: Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ServiceTitan for estimates and jobs, Quo for calls and texts, and your Google Ads and Google Business Profile. If you don't have a system yet, getting you set up on Jobber and Quo is part of your setup.
03Who owns my Google Ads and software accounts?
You do, always. Your ad account, phone number, and customer list stay in your name. If we ever part ways, everything stays with you.
04Am I locked into a contract?
No. It's month to month. If you don't like the results, you can leave. We'd rather earn the next month.
05Do I have to fire my current agency?
No. We can audit their work and hand you our notes, or run the automation side while they keep your ads. Plenty of owners start that way.
