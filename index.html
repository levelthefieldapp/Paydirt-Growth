Questions

What owners ask first.

01Can Watchdog change my ads?
Not on the $39 plan. Read only means Google lets us look, not touch. With Plus (coming soon), owners who run their own ads can approve one-click fixes and undo them. Plus never raises your budget and never touches an account an agency runs.
02Do I have to tell my agency?
Up to you. We never contact them. They may see our read-only login in your account's user list.
03What if it flags something that isn't waste?
Tap "This isn't waste." It comes out of your totals and won't come back.
04What if it doesn't find anything?
Then you're in good shape. And if Watchdog doesn't find more waste than its price in your first 30 days, that month is free.
05How fast do I get my Leak Report?
Within 2 business days of giving access, by email. No login needed.
06Who sees my data?
Paydirt and the services that run Watchdog, each named in our Privacy Policy. We never sell your data.
07Are you an agency trying to win my account?
Paydirt Growth, which runs Watchdog, also manages Google Ads for some trade businesses. We only bring it up if you ask.
